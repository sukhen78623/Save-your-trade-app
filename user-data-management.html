Your account

User data management

This page explains how Save Your Trade handles the information linked to your account, and how you can ask us about it. It is shown only after you log in.

Your account

You are signed in as your account.

You can update your name, email and password from Settings → User profile inside the journal.

What is stored for your account

  • Account details: your name, email, gender and date of birth, as given when you signed up.
  • Your journal: the setups, notes, mistakes and screenshots you add to your trades.
  • Broker trades: read-only trade data fetched from the broker you connect, such as Zerodha or Upstox.
  • Plan status: which plan your account is on.

How your data is used

Your data is used only to run your journal: showing your trades and statistics, syncing with your broker, keeping your account working and answering your support requests.

We do not use your user data to send promotional emails.

Broker connections

Save Your Trade only reads trade data from your broker. It does not place, change, cancel or close any order on your behalf.

Where your data is stored

Your login and trade data are stored with Supabase, which we use as our service infrastructure.

AI-summarised report (Google Gemini)

The Overall report in your Analytics is summarised with AI. We use Google's Gemini AI model to group the mistakes you note in your journal and to write a short summary of what is going well and what to improve.

What is sent to Gemini: the short mistake notes our app cannot group by itself, up to 25 of your recent trade notes (shortened, with email addresses and long numbers removed), and summary figures for the period you are viewing, such as trade counts, profit or loss, win rate and your discipline counts.

What is not sent: your name, email address, account details, scrip or instrument names, broker details, individual trade prices and quantities, setup names, screenshots or payment information.

This information is processed by Google under its own terms and privacy policy for the Gemini API. The report text is kept only in your own browser, not on our servers, so it is cleared if you clear your browser data or use a different device. AI can make mistakes, so please treat it as a guide, not financial advice. Full details are in our Privacy policy.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and data. Tap a button below and send the email that opens.

Your responsibility: please keep the details you give us accurate and keep your login details private.
